## Strategic conclusion

Vibroxi should not compete with consumer platforms on low price or fast household booking, nor with integrated FM providers on the full facility-management bundle. Its strongest proposed position is a specialist commercial painting, wood polishing and finishing execution partner for defined projects, subcontracted work packages and planned recurring property work.

## Current SERP and pricing-content observations — 12 November 2026

Recent Delhi NCR search results show several useful patterns in pages competing for painting and wood-polishing price queries:

- Delhi Painting Company publishes separate commercial office painting and wood-polishing cost guides. The office guide segments by space type and explains paintable area, surface condition, access and working hours. Its wood guide explains finish categories, new versus old surfaces, measurement and preparation. Use this topic coverage as a checklist for buyer questions, not its published price figures or performance claims.

### Application to Vibroxi

1. Create one canonical pricing page for commercial project estimates rather than publishing multiple near-duplicate price pages.
2. Do not copy consumer/residential price tables into a commercial contractor website. Do not present third-party rates as Vibroxi rates.
3. Explain quote variables in detail: work volume, surface measurement basis, service type, substrate condition, material and finish specification, coats, preparation, height/access, occupancy, protection, work hours, phasing and mobilisation.
4. Make quote inclusions explicit: labour, materials, primer/consumables, access equipment, protection, waste handling, inspection and handover must be listed as included, excluded or client-supplied.
5. Use service pages to answer their own technical and buyer questions; use the pricing page to consolidate quotation intent and link back to all core services.
6. Build supporting guides only when they answer a distinct question and do not cannibalise the canonical service or pricing page.

Competitor sources are research references only. Their prices, claims, case studies, customer reviews, credentials, images and guarantees are not Vibroxi evidence.
