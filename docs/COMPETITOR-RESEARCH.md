# Competitor website research — commercial finishing

Research is used for positioning, information architecture and buyer experience. Competitor claims, images, reviews and statistics are not Vibroxi evidence and must not be copied.

## Large brands and platforms

### Asian Paints / Beautiful Homes
- Website: https://www.asianpaints.com/
- Service reference: https://www.asianpaints.com/services.html
- Wood solutions: https://www.asianpaints.com/services/wood-solutions.html
- Useful patterns: clearly separated service categories, material and finish education, process explanation, consultation and quality assurance presentation.
- Vibroxi application: explain surface preparation, finish systems, sample approvals, site protection and handover for commercial buyers. Do not mimic consumer design consultation or imply brand affiliation.

### Berger Express Painting
- Website: https://www.bergerpaints.com/express-painting
- Useful patterns: site consultation, defined execution stages, mechanised process explanation, protection, cleanup and after-service information.
- Vibroxi application: show a credible project workflow from site review through scope, execution and handover. Only claim tools, cleanup standards or aftercare actually provided.

### Urban Company
- Website: https://www.urbancompany.com/
- Delhi NCR: https://www.urbancompany.com/delhi-ncr
- Useful patterns: service taxonomy, location architecture, clear booking/enquiry paths and service-specific pages.
- Vibroxi application: use the taxonomy and navigation discipline, but avoid quick-booking, mass-market and small-job positioning.

### NoBroker Home Services
- Website: https://www.nobroker.in/home-services
- Useful patterns: explain service inclusions and exclusions and reduce ambiguity before booking.
- Vibroxi application: provide scope clarity, BOQ review, material responsibility, access arrangements and exclusions in commercial project enquiries.

### HomeTriangle
- Website: https://hometriangle.com/
- Services: https://hometriangle.com/all-services
- Useful patterns: category discovery and simple service navigation.
- Vibroxi application: make it easy for procurement and property teams to find the relevant specialist service without turning the site into a general household services marketplace.

### Livspace
- Website: https://www.livspace.com/in
- Useful patterns: premium visual presentation, project storytelling and consultation-led conversion.
- Vibroxi application: use polished project photography and factual case studies. Do not offer or claim interior design, redesign or construction.

## Strategic conclusion

Vibroxi should not compete with consumer platforms on low price or fast household booking, nor with integrated FM providers on the full facility-management bundle. Its strongest proposed position is a specialist commercial painting, wood polishing and finishing execution partner for defined projects, subcontracted work packages and planned recurring property work.
