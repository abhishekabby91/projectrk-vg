# Vibroxi SEO and website implementation roadmap

## Positioning baseline
- Primary market: India, with Delhi NCR as the initial service region.
- Primary buyer: facility management companies, commercial property owners/operators, hotels and commercial project contractors.
- Commercial model: direct commercial projects, specialist subcontracting work packages and planned recurring property maintenance.
- Service identity: execution and finishing contractor. Do not position as an interior design, redesign, architecture or construction company.
- India uses the root website. Do not create an /in/ prefix. Future country sections such as /us/ or /uk/ should be introduced only after genuine market entry and locally relevant content exists.
- Brand and domain remain provisional. Do not publish guessed canonical URLs or production-domain structured data. Set NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL only after the owner confirms the purchased domain.

## Technical SEO decisions
1. No production domain is assumed. Canonical metadata and Organization/WebSite structured data remain inactive until NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL is set.
2. The sitemap lists only pages intended for indexing. The projects page remains noindex until real, permission-cleared case studies exist.
3. Legacy service URLs are redirected to consolidated canonical service pages.
4. Do not create thin city pages for every NCR locality. Expand location content only when there is genuine service capacity and unique local information.
5. Avoid fake LocalBusiness address, phone, opening hours, reviews, ratings, client logos, certifications, project counts or guarantees.
6. Use real image assets with descriptive alt text and permission to publish. Avoid using competitor images.
7. Maintain one clear H1 per page, unique titles/descriptions, descriptive internal links and useful page-specific copy.
8. Keep redirects, sitemap entries, canonical tags and internal links aligned whenever routes change.

## Conversion and trust priorities
- Make project enquiry the primary action: Discuss a commercial project or Request a site assessment.
- Qualify enquiries by company, buyer type, location, property type, approximate area/site count, required services, engagement type and start date.
- Connect the enquiry form to a verified business email or CRM before launch. Current form is only a front-end demo and does not transmit submissions.
- Publish genuine case studies only with verified scope, accurate outcomes, approved photographs and client permission.
- Confirm actual service capability, electrical qualifications, safety documentation, insurance, vendor onboarding readiness and delivery capacity before making claims.
- Add a clear scope/BOQ process, material responsibility, access assumptions, exclusions, inspection and handover information.
