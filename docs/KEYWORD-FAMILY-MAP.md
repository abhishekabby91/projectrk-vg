## Cannibalisation and quality rules

- One canonical URL per intent; do not create multiple pages for near-identical keyword synonyms.
- Do not add separate city + service pages until the location has unique content and genuine local project capability.
- Avoid repeating the same city list in every paragraph. Use natural terms in titles, H1, intro, subheadings, body and internal links.
- Do not use hidden keyword lists or repetitive exact-match phrases.
- FAQs must answer actual buyer questions and be visibly rendered.
- Competitor page structure can inform topic coverage; never copy their prose, photos, reviews, prices, guarantees or credentials.
- Measure impressions, clicks, CTR and query-to-page mapping in Search Console after indexing; update based on evidence, not assumed ranking promises.
